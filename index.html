Q: What should I do if a URL is flagged as CRITICAL?

A: Immediately sever connection to the domain. Do not input credentials, passwords, or 2FA tokens. If an employee opened the link, quarantine the workstation and initiate credential rotation.

Q: How does Error Level Analysis (ELA) detect synthetic deepfake images?

A: Natural photographs have uniform JPEG compression error rates across the whole sensor. Synthetic inpainting or spliced faces show distinct contrast halos when re-compressed at 95% quality.
